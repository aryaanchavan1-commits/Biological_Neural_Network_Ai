Robustness Evaluation

Robustness testing evaluates how well the network performs under adverse conditions. Gaussian noise adds random perturbations to inputs. Salt-and-pepper noise randomly zeroes or saturates features. Feature dropout randomly removes input features. Higher robustness means the network degrades gracefully rather than failing catastrophically.
