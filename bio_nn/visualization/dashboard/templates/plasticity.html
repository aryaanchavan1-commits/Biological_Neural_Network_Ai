Synaptic plasticity is the biological process by which connections strengthen or weaken based on activity. STDP (Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity) adjusts weights based on the relative timing of pre- and post-synaptic spikes — "neurons that fire together wire together." Homeostatic plasticity scales weights to maintain stable activity levels.
