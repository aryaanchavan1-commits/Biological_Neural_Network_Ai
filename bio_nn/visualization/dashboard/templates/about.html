About BIO-NN

A modular research framework for biologically-inspired neural networks.

BIO-NN is a modular research framework for investigating whether biologically-inspired neural network mechanisms can improve continual learning, robustness, and computational efficiency. It provides a configurable platform where each biological mechanism can be independently enabled, disabled, and parameterized to measure its contribution to network performance.

Biological Plausibility Levels

Level 0
Pure Engineering Abstraction
Standard ANNs with no biological motivation. ReLU activations, dense layers, backpropagation.
Level 1
Loosely Biologically Inspired
Architectures loosely inspired by biology: CNNs (visual cortex), attention (selective attention).
Level 2
Computationally Biologically Motivated
Mechanisms motivated by biological computation but not constrained to match real neurons.
Level 3
Biologically Plausible Approximation
Models that approximate biological constraints while remaining computationally tractable.
Level 4
Closely Constrained by Biological Evidence
Models constrained by measured biological parameters, suitable for neuroscience validation.

Disclaimer

This is a research framework for computational experimentation. It does not claim to recreate the human brain or create AGI. Biological inspiration is used as a source of architectural ideas, not as a claim of biological realism.
