Structural plasticity allows the network to physically reorganize — growing new connections where they're needed and pruning unused ones. This mirrors biological neurogenesis and synaptic pruning. Activity-based growth adds connections between co-active neurons, while weight-based pruning removes weak connections to maintain sparsity.
